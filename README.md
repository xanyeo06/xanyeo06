# Xander Yeo

**Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics** · Ngee Ann Polytechnic, Singapore

A final year student at Ngee Ann Polytechnic, studying cybersecurity and digital forensics. Most of what I enjoy sits on the defensive side — finding an attack in a pile of logs, then building something that finds it for you.

The repositories here are mostly coursework. There's a SOC triage tool that reads network traffic and flags what looks malicious, a classifier that takes raw log lines and works out how urgent they are, and a Splunk lab where I attacked a fictional company and then wrote the detections to catch myself. I also spent longer than I should have comparing anomaly detection models on credit card fraud data, mostly learning how often a model that looks good on paper falls apart in practice.

There's cloud and networking work too: an AWS setup with auto scaling, a Palo Alto firewall across two sites, and a mobile forensics case that hinged on a calculator app which turned out to be something else entirely.
